# Third-party assets

- **PyBullet Panda collision meshes.** The eight OBJ files used in the diagnostic plots come from the [PyBullet Panda asset directory](https://github.com/bulletphysics/bullet3/tree/d3afe76132fc76b0f9b2359cbaef47a3766e7fbb/examples/pybullet/gym/pybullet_data/franka_panda). Their Apache 2.0 license is reproduced in [figure_sources/panda_meshes/LICENSE.txt](figure_sources/panda_meshes/LICENSE.txt).
- **CaDeLaC Panda model.** The constraint-free MuJoCo model was derived from [CaDeLaC revision a9b889c](https://github.com/Schulze18/cadelac/tree/a9b889c638b749bd58b337ed66ba38896ad01477). The archived derivative preserves the inertial and passive dynamics used for the reported replays. The upstream MIT notice is in [evidence/followup/CADelac_LICENSE.txt](evidence/followup/CADelac_LICENSE.txt). The offscreen renderings in Fig. 1(a) use the Panda display meshes supplied with the same revision. Their Apache 2.0 notice is in [figure_sources/CADELAC_PANDA_VISUAL_LICENSE.txt](figure_sources/CADELAC_PANDA_VISUAL_LICENSE.txt).
- **Trajectory data.** Yang and Ko distribute the [Panda trajectories](https://doi.org/10.17632/df4kfzrbrg.1) through Mendeley Data under CC BY 4.0. The repository includes the study's alignment-screen receipt with the group identifiers and applicable thresholds.
- **NeuralActuator data.** The archive includes a coverage summary for the Franka Panda subset. The source records are distributed at [the dataset repository](https://huggingface.co/datasets/frankzydou/NAD).
